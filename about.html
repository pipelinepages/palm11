About Palm 11

We exist to energize communities and power tomorrow with solar solutions that are reliable, sustainable and built for the long term.

Our Story

Rooted in Purpose, Driven by Progress

Palm 11 Energy Limited was founded with a clear conviction: access to clean, dependable electricity should not be a privilege. It should be a foundation for stronger communities and a more resilient future.

We combine deep technical expertise with a client-first approach. Every project we undertake is designed to deliver measurable performance, transparent communication and lasting value — whether for a single family home or a multi-megawatt commercial installation.

Our name reflects our commitment: the palm as a symbol of resilience and growth, and “11” as a mark of excellence and forward momentum.

Purpose

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To design, deliver and support high-quality solar energy systems that empower homes, businesses and communities with reliable, affordable and sustainable power — while setting a new standard for professionalism and accountability in the renewable energy sector.

Our Vision

A future where clean energy is the default choice across Africa — powering progress, protecting the environment and strengthening the communities we serve. We aim to be the trusted partner that makes this transition practical, profitable and permanent.
